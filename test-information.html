Diagnostic Test Information

About the Diagnostic Process

The Urine Test Strip Reader System is an AI-enabled healthcare solution that analyzes urine samples using colorimetric test strips. The system helps healthcare professionals detect abnormalities and monitor patient health conditions efficiently.

Important urine parameters such as Glucose, Protein, Ketones, Blood, and Bilirubin are evaluated to assist in diagnosis.

Special attention should be given to abnormal glucose levels, protein concentration, and presence of blood in urine, as these may indicate underlying medical conditions.

Patients should always follow healthcare instructions and consult qualified medical professionals before interpreting diagnostic results.

Units and Chemical Representation

Glucose concentration may be reported in mg/dL*.

Water is represented as H2O and carbon dioxide as CO2.

Medical Abbreviations

UTI, RBC, WBC, pH, and SG are commonly used in urine analysis reports.
